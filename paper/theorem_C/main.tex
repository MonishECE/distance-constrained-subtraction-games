\begin{abstract}We prove the universal row recurrence and closed-form run hierarchy. Twelve parameter intervals from $A=14$ to $A=21846$ are certified by exact finite windows plus induction, covering $10323$ pairs. We explain adjacent lower-boundary failures and record $K_{13}=20025$, with deficit $d_{13}=1821$. A parameter-uniform proof, a closed upper-endpoint formula, and global exclusion of additional intervals remain Open.\end{abstract}
