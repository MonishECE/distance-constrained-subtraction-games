# Manuscripts

`main.tex` integrates the game definition, Theorems A/B/C, the verification-methodology section, evidence tables, limitations and references. The finalized theorem statements live together in `theorems/` (`theorem_A.tex`, `theorem_B.tex`, `theorem_C.tex`). `theorem_A/main.tex`, `theorem_B/main.tex` and `theorem_C/main.tex` include their prerequisites and can be compiled separately from their own folders. Shared formatting and references are in `shared/`. Per-theorem method notes and verification scripts are in `../methodology/` and are included in `main.tex`.

Compile twice with pdfLaTeX to resolve references. Single-file equivalents are in `../overleaf/`. The LaTeX sources are authoritative; compile them to produce PDFs.
