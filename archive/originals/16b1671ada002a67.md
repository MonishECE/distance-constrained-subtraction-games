# Source and citation audit (checked October 9, 2026)

1. **R. Sprague (1935)**, *Über mathematische Kampfspiele*, *Tôhoku Mathematical Journal*, vol. 41, pp. 438–444. **Verified** against the original J-STAGE journal listing: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/tmj1911/41/0/41_0_438/_article . Note that some later bibliographies say 1936; the journal's online listing uses 1935 and the volume indexes label 1935/36. We use the journal's listed year.
2. **P. M. Grundy (1939)**, *Mathematics and Games*, *Eureka* 2, pp. 6–8. **Bibliographical metadata verified** from OEIS's entry with a scan link: https://oeis.org/A002188/a002188.pdf . No argument from its historical scan is claimed as part of our proof.
3. **E. R. Berlekamp, J. H. Conway, R. K. Guy (2001)**, *Winning Ways for Your Mathematical Plays*, volume 1, second edition. **Verified** against the publisher and the author's publication list: https://www.routledge.com/Winning-Ways-for-Your-Mathematical-Plays-Volume-1-2nd-Edition/Berlekamp-Conway-Guy/p/book/9781568811307 ; https://math.berkeley.edu/~berlek/pubs/books.html . The publisher lists volume 1 as 296 pages inclusive of front matter; page counts differ by catalog conventions, so none is asserted in the bibliography.
4. **V. Gurvich (January 2012)**, *On Repetition-free Subtraction Games and Vile-dopey Integers*, RUTCOR report RRR-01-2012. **Publication details verified** against the author's Rutgers publication list, entry 240: https://rutcor.rutgers.edu/~gurvich/publist.pdf . A third-party hosted report text was found; because it is not authoritative, its detailed claims are not used. This report is adjacent prior art, **not** a proof of the distance-m=1 game.

These sources are **background, not imported proofs**. The self-contained A/B/C arguments should still receive independent mathematical review.
