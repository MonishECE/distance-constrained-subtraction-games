\subsection{Theorem B: finite-certificate method}\label{sec:methodB}
Each fixed $k$ is proved by the same two finite premises plus induction. Lemma~\ref{lem:Bwindow}: if $k$ consecutive P-state rows agree at offsets $t$ and $t+p$, determinism of the Theorem~A recurrence forces all later rows to agree. Lemma~\ref{lem:Bbridge}: starting outcomes then repeat from $t+k$, so checking the proposed formula through $t+k+p-1$ establishes it for every $n$.
The script \texttt{methodology/theorem\_B/verify\_theorems\_ab.py} computes each $Q_n$ with exact integer bitsets, finds the earliest window witness $t$ in $[0,2p]$, verifies the residue formula over the bridge range, and cross-checks every row and starting outcome through the bridge against both an independent direct Sprague--Grundy solver and naive legal-move quantification. Minimality of the period is checked by exhausting all nonzero cyclic shifts. The reproduced windows are recorded in \texttt{data/certificates/theorem\_B\_audit.csv} (Table~\ref{tab:Bcert}).
